Chiang Mai, Thailand with Miko and Kuro

2/14/2026

0 Comments

 
🐕‍🦺 Miko
🐈‍⬛ ​Kuro
Jan. 2026
4 days
This housesit was a unique situation. For three weeks prior, we cared for the neighbor's cats in the house right next door, so we had the opportunity to get to know Rosana (pet parent), Miko, and Kuro (a bit! He's shy) for several weeks. We shared meals and enjoyed relaxed evenings together. 

Rosana was very thorough in preparing for our 4-day stay and showed us tasks related to caring for Miko and Kuro during the weeks before.

Miko is a beautiful border collie mix. As with border collies, she is smart and a bit stubborn. It was obvious she missed her mother as border collies are known to be loyal, but she behaved well with us sitters.

Every morning, I steamed chicken, vegetables, and an egg and hand fed her. We went for walks around the garden and played with her squeaky ball. Miko is a happy girl who enjoys being outside with her friend, Daisy (a sweet Thai dog who stays in the area), and is easygoing. 

One challenge we faced is that there are three soi dogs who come by the neighbor's for food every day. They are gentle with humans, but unpredictable with dogs. We monitored to make sure the dogs did not come into our yard. Luckily, everyone remained safe.

Kuro is a shy and independent outdoor cat. We thought it may be difficult to get close with him, but he did warm up to us within a day and we were able to pet him as well as put on/take off his harness (both pets have a GPS tracker on them).

It was a short housesit, but we enjoyed getting to know them for the weeks prior as well.

We were in touch with the pet parents for several months prior to sitting for them. Unfortunately, another housesitter let them down so they thought they would be done with hosting petsitters after 20 years of good experiences.

However, they decided to give housesitting one more chance and trusted us. We visited their home and met Remy and Misha, two fluffy and sweet Ragdolls, and we agreed to care for them for three weeks in followed by caring for the next door neighbor's pets immediately after (more details on that in the next post).

It's important to keep up with cleaning with fluffy pets in warm environments!

We also appreciated how everything in the house was clearly labeled and there was plenty of pet supplies stocked up. 

Overall, it was a smooth housesit.

I (Lianne) looked after Mogu the bunny and Bbuyo the dwarf hamster for 10 days in a small town in Gyeongnam-do, South Korea. I’ve grown up with hamsters and have petsat for a caged rabbit before, but this was my first time caring for a free-roaming bunny with a whole room to explore.

Thanks to the pet parents’ clear and detailed instructions, daily care went smoothly: feeding both dry and fresh food, refreshing their water, changing the litter, vacuuming the bunny’s room now and then (and soothing with a treat after the noise!), monitoring the room temperature, etc. I made sure Bbuyo’s cage stayed clean and comfortable, and of course, gave Mogu plenty of attention and pets! While we usually don’t allow indoor cameras (as per TrustedHousesitters.com policy), I was fine with the setup in this case since the camera was only focused on the pets, and it gave the owners peace of mind. I still sent daily updates and photos, as always.
​
It was truly a pleasure caring for Mogu and Bbuyo. Thankful for Chrissi and Mike being generous and kind hosts with excellent communication. They made me feel welcome and arranged my round trip transportation which was really appreciated. I’d happily care for them again anytime.

6/8/2025

0 Comments

 
🐈 Meredith
🐈‍⬛ Penny
🐈 Nettie
31, May - 3, June 2025
3 days
After several years of hopping from one housesit to another, we began to feel the strain and needed a break. To recharge, we settled into a short-term rental in Bangkok for a few months. While the stability of staying in one place was refreshing, we found ourselves missing the company of animals. Our evening routine turned into wandering the neighborhood or Lumphini Park to feed / play with stray cats. 

So when a last-minute local housesit opportunity came up for a weekend in Bangkok, we didn’t hesitate. It was the perfect chance to be around cats again, explore a new neighborhood, and continue building our experience and client base. 

These three gorgeous cats each have a unique personality, but all were easy to care for and love. The youngest, Nettie, had just been spayed and was recovering in our room, separated from her sisters to protect her sutures. Despite wearing a cone, she still managed to remove the bandage a few times, so we closely monitored to ensure the wound stayed clean and covered, all while showering her with chin scratches and attention. We also administered her medication twice a day. Penny and Meredith are more independent and shy, especially Penny, who preferred to watch us at a distance from above. We managed to gain their trust with treats and letting them approach us when they felt ready.  

The hosts were welcoming and communicative. Overall, the petsit was smooth and enjoyable!
