Perks of a Housesitting Couple

4/21/2024

0 Comments

 
As housesitting gains popularity, the decision of who cares for your home and pets becomes crucial, requiring a significant level of trust. While solo housesitters offer their own advantages, we believe there's something special about having a couple or family stay with your pets in your home. Here's why:
​
  • Double the attention: More hands, more love! With two of us, your pets receive double the attention and affection they crave.
  • Emergency Coverage: Life happens, and when it does, having a couple housesit ensures continuity of care. While housesitting in Australia once, one of us had to go to the US for a brief time, but the other stayed behind to provide uninterrupted care.
  • Safety on Walks: In areas with stray animals, like Thailand, having two people allows us to manage unexpected situations during walks more effectively, ensuring the safety of your pets.
  • Built-in Companionship: Long-term housesits can get lonely for solo sitters. Perhaps solo sitters may be more likely to go out to meet people or request to invite unknown guests to your home. As a couple, we provide each other with companionship, eliminating the need to seek external company and we can spend more time at home with your pets.

Trust in us to care for your home and pets like they're our own. Reach out to discuss how we can make your next housesit stress-free and enjoyable!
